Sel Roti

⭐ 4.7 ⏱ 30 Minutes 🍽 Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons ghee
  • 1/2 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • Cooking oil for frying
  • Water as required

Instructions

  1. Soak the rice in water for several hours.
  2. Drain the water and grind the rice into a smooth batter.
  3. Add sugar, milk, ghee and cardamom powder.
  4. Mix everything well to make a thick but pourable batter.
  5. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  6. Pour the batter into the hot oil in a circular ring shape.
  7. Fry both sides until golden brown and crispy.
  8. Remove from oil and serve warm.
