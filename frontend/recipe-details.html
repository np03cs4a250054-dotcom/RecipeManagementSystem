Chicken Momo

⭐ 4.8 ⏱ 45 Minutes 🍽 Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 250g minced chicken
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Prepare the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Mix chicken, onion, garlic, and spices together.
  3. Roll the dough into small circles.
  4. Place filling in each wrapper.
  5. Seal the momos carefully.
  6. Steam for 15-20 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with Nepali achar.

Nutrition Facts

Protein: 20g

Carbohydrates: 30g

Fat: 12g
