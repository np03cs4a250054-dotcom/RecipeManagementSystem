Chatamari

⭐ 4.7 ⏱ 25 Minutes 🍽 Serves 3

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 150g minced meat
  • 1 egg
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 green chili
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Mix rice flour and water to prepare a smooth batter.
  2. Mix minced meat with onion, tomato, chili and spices.
  3. Heat a flat pan and lightly grease it with oil.
  4. Pour a thin layer of rice batter onto the pan.
  5. Add the meat mixture on top.
  6. Crack an egg over the mixture.
  7. Cover the pan and cook until the rice base becomes crispy.
  8. Garnish with coriander.
  9. Serve hot with achar.
