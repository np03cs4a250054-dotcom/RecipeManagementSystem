Thukpa

⭐ 4.6 ⏱ 35 Minutes 🍽 Serves 3

Ingredients

  • 200g noodles
  • 150g chicken
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 cabbage
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot.
  2. Add chopped onion, garlic and ginger.
  3. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add carrots, cabbage and other vegetables.
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and bring it to a boil.
  6. Add noodles and cook until soft.
  7. Add soy sauce, salt and pepper.
  8. Cook for a few more minutes.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Nutrition Facts

Calories: 320 kcal

Protein: 18g

Carbohydrates: 42g

Fat: 8g
