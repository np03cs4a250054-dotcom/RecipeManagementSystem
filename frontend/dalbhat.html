Dal Bhat

⭐ 4.9 ⏱ 40 Minutes 🍽 Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 cup red or yellow lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and cook it with water until soft.
  2. Wash the lentils and cook them with turmeric and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  4. Add onion, garlic and tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Add the cooked mixture to the lentils and simmer for a few minutes.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander.
  7. Serve hot dal with steamed rice, vegetables and pickle.

Nutrition Facts

Calories: 450 kcal

Protein: 16g

Carbohydrates: 75g

Fat: 10g
