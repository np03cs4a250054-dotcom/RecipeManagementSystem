Aloo Tama

⭐ 4.5 ⏱ 35 Minutes 🍽 Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes
  • 1 cup bamboo shoots
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 2 green chilies
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Peel and cut the potatoes into medium-sized pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  3. Add onion, garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant.
  4. Add turmeric powder and chopped tomatoes.
  5. Add the potatoes and mix well.
  6. Add bamboo shoots and green chilies.
  7. Pour in enough water to cover the ingredients.
  8. Cover and cook until the potatoes become soft.
  9. Add salt and adjust the seasoning.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
